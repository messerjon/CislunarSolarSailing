## Q2 — Launch inclination

- From GTO: transit time varies only 4% across inclinations (434–450 d). Lowest achievable arrival-plane offset from the Moon's orbit: 6° at i = 28.5° (with RAAN/launch time chosen).
- From LEO 1000 km: transit time varies only 4% across inclinations (943–979 d). Lowest achievable arrival-plane offset from the Moon's orbit: 7° at i = 18.3° (with RAAN/launch time chosen).
- Recommendation: choose inclination to match the lunar plane (≈ lunar inclination to the equator at launch epoch) and pick launch time (RAAN) for plane alignment; transit time is insensitive.

## Q4 — Lunar south-pole sitter

- Minimum acceleration to hover at any point with the south pole ≥10° above the horizon: 0.31 mm/s², mostly *out of the lunar orbit plane*, which a sail (Sun ≈ in plane) supplies at ≤ ⅓ efficiency.
- No static sail-only pole-sitter exists up to a_c = 1.5 mm/s² (practical ceiling for CP1 film).
- Hybrid: sail covers part of the demand; SEP must still supply ≈ 5.7–8.1 km/s per year (≈ 32–42 kg Xe per 100 kg per year at 1500 s).
- Implication: use periodic south-pole-viewing orbits (southern L1/L2 halos / NRHO, sail-displaced periodic orbits) rather than a static pole-sitter.

## Q8 — Lowest acceptable demonstration orbit (lowest altitude with ≥ +10 km net raise in 30 d, 28.5°)

| a_c (mm/s²) | Solar min | Moderate | Solar max |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.05 | 550 km | 650 km | 800 km |
| 0.1 | 550 km | 650 km | 800 km |
| 0.2 | 550 km | 600 km | 800 km |
| 0.3 | 550 km | 650 km | 800 km |
| 0.5 | 550 km | 650 km | 850 km |

Sail drag and SRP both scale with A/m, so the crossover altitude is nearly independent of sail size; it is set by thermospheric density (solar cycle).

- Double-sided sail vs conventional (a_c 0.2, moderate): 650 km vs 600 km
- Retractable sail vs conventional (a_c 0.2, moderate): 550 km vs 600 km
- Hybrid (gimballed SEP) vs conventional (a_c 0.2, moderate): 500 km vs 600 km
- A single-sided sail's absorptive (Cr) back face can fly edge-on to the airflow while still taking photon momentum along the light direction; a fully specular double-sided sail cannot decouple thrust from drag, so it performs worse in drag-limited LEO.

## Attitude / momentum management

- A 0.5% CP–CM offset produces 3.6 mN·m (309 N·m·s/day): far beyond reaction-wheel capacity, so continuous trim is mandatory.
- Gimballed-bus CM shift: 17° on a 1 m arm. Electrochromic film: 8% of sail area at ΔCn = 0.5 (5% at 0.8). Both scale with SRP, so authority holds at every cone angle.
- Electrochromic films trim pitch/yaw continuously (no wheel desaturation); roll about the sail normal still needs wheels, vanes, or twisted/angled film elements.
