How the model flies the mission

1 · Earth spiral

Orbit-averaged Gauss equations in modified equinoctial elements, stepped with RK4. At each of 48 points per orbit the sail takes the locally optimal attitude that raises apogee fastest, with perigee protection, using the non-ideal optical model of the chosen film. Earth's shadow, J2 and NRLMSIS drag (for perigees below 2,500 km) are included, and SEP adds thrust within its gimbal cone until the propellant runs out.

The spiral ends when the osculating orbit reaches the cislunar gateway energy, C ≈ 3.29.

2 · Energy matching

The state is handed to the Earth–Moon circular restricted three-body problem in the rotating frame. The Sun line comes from the ephemeris, with optional solar gravity. The sail steers to change the Jacobi constant as fast as possible until it equals the destination's value.

3 · Phasing and capture

The sail then feathers and the spacecraft coasts. The best point of that coast starts the capture leg, which is modelled, not optimised: phasing plus capture take the study's natural coast time for the pair of regions (Q3: T = Tenergy + Tcoast). It is drawn dashed.

4 · Legs between stations

From a station the sail repeats steps 2 and 3, leaving on the best of five departure days. Two kinds of leg defeat the simple steering law: leaving the NRHO toward a halo drives perilune into the Moon, and legs that start at L4 or L5 stall. Those are estimated from the reverse leg using the three-body problem's time-reversal symmetry, as the study did, and drawn dashed.

Payload and link

Instruments sit on the anti-Sun (+n) or Sun (−n) face; the membrane blocks ±5° about the sail plane. The bus rolls freely about the sail normal (thrust does not depend on roll). The downlink uses 1 dB line loss, 0.8 dB atmosphere and polarization loss, a Gaussian main lobe (12 (θ/θ3dB)² dB), BPSK with concatenated coding (Eb/N0 2.5 dB at 10−6) and a 3 dB margin. The Moon can occult the Earth. Away from the propulsive phases the sail is shown facing the Sun, an illustrative station-keeping attitude.

Fidelity

  • The engine is a line-by-line port of the study's Python simulator. Steering tables, ephemerides, averaged rates and spiral times match it (841 days for the CR-S spiral from a perigee-raised GTO).
  • The Moon follows a circular orbit inclined 5.145° to the ecliptic with the 18.6-year node cycle. Shadows are cylindrical.
  • "Align with lunar plane" picks the launch RAAN so that, after J2 drift, the spiral arrives in the Moon's orbit plane.
  • Times are first-order estimates for concept studies, not flight trajectories.
